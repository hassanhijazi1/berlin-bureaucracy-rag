Section 20 
Foreigners seeking employment following stays in the federal territory 
(1) In order to look for a job 
1. 
foreigners are granted a temporary residence permit, if they successfully 
completed a course of study in the federal territory as part of a stay under sections 16b or 
16c, 
2. 
foreigners are granted a temporary residence permit, if they finished their 
research activities as part of a stay under sections 18d or 18f, 
3. 
foreigners are to be granted a temporary residence permit, if they successfully 
completed quality vocational training in the federal territory as part of a stay under section 
16a, 
4. 
foreigners are to be granted a temporary residence permit once equivalence of 
the vocational qualification has been established or the authorisation to work in the 
occupation in the federal territory has been granted as part of a stay under section 16d, 
5. 
foreigners are granted a temporary residence permit after successfully 
completing training as an assistant or helper in a state-recognised or similarly regulated 
occupation in the field of health care in the federal territory, provided foreigners are 
permitted to take up the employment in question in accordance with sections 18a, 18b, 
18d, 18g, 19c and 21. 
(2) The temporary residence permit is granted pursuant to subsection (1) on the condition 
that the foreigner’s subsistence is secured. In the cases covered by subsection (1) nos. 1 to 
4, the temporary residence permit is granted for a period of up to 18 months. In the cases 
covered by subsection (1) no. 5, it is granted for a period of up to 12 months and may be 
extended once by up to six months if the foreigner’s subsistence is secured. It may not be 
extended beyond these maximum periods.
