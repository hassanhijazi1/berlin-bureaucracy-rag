Section 18 
Registration certificate 
(1) The registration authority provides the data subject with a written or electronic 
registration certificate at his or her request. The registration certificate contains the 
following data: 
1. 
family name, 
2. 
given names, indicating name usually used, 
3. 
doctoral degree, 
4. 
date of birth, 
5. 
current addresses, indicating principal and secondary residence. 
The person required to register is required to transfer his or her family name, given 
names, date of birth and address of the current principal or sole residence to that 
end. 
(2) At the data subject’s request, the registration certificate may, in addition to the 
data referred to in subsection (1) sentence 2, also contain data as referred to in 

Service provided by the Federal Ministry of Justice and Consumer Protection 
and the Federal Office of Justice ‒ www.gesetze-im-internet.de

section 3 (1) nos. 2, 5 to 16 and 17, with the exception of the blocking code and 
blocking sum of the national identity card and eID card, as well as the fact that no

(3) The electronic registration certificate is issued free of charge. 
(4) In all other cases, section 10 (2) and (3) and section 11 (2) nos. 1 to 3 apply 
accordingly.
