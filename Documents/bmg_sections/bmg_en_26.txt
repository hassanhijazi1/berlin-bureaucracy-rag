Section 26 
Exemption from the registration requirement 
The registration requirement under section 17 (1) and (2) does not apply to 
1. 
members of a foreign diplomatic mission or foreign consular representation and 
family members living with them in the same household if the persons referred to do 
not have German nationality, are not permanently resident in Germany and are not 
in gainful employment, 
2. 
persons for whom this exemption has been stipulated in international treaties. 
Exemption from the registration requirement under sentence 1 no. 1 applies only on 
the basis of reciprocity.
